The pace lock

This lock exists to be respectful. The pace can be changed. It cannot go past 480 requests per hour, a gap under 1 second, or a burst above 8, unless you accept that in a signed commit.

Este candado existe para ser respetuoso. El ritmo se puede cambiar. No puede pasar de 480 peticiones por hora, un hueco menor de 1 segundo, o una ráfaga mayor de 8, salvo que lo aceptes en un commit firmado.

The numbers live in your own copy, in config/prod/rate_limiter.yaml. There is no account on this page.

Los números viven en tu propia copia, en config/prod/rate_limiter.yaml. Esta página no tiene cuenta.

To go past the lock, add rate_override.yml at the root of your copy, with one of these lines, and sign the commit:

responsibility: I accept responsibility for requests above the respectful pace.
responsibility: Acepto la responsabilidad por peticiones por encima del ritmo respetuoso.

An Action refuses the change when that commit is not signed, or when the line is missing.
